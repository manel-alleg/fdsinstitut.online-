FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What level do I need to start? +

We welcome students from A1 (complete beginner) to C2. Our placement test ensures you join the right group — you'll be challenged but never lost.

How do live sessions work? +

Sessions run 3× per week on Zoom, 90 minutes each. All sessions are recorded and automatically added to your Moodle library within hours.

Can I pause or cancel? +

Yes. You can pause your subscription for up to one month or cancel with 7 days notice before your next billing cycle. No penalties, no fees.

Do you offer exam preparation? +

Absolutely. We have dedicated tracks for TestDaF, Goethe-Zertifikat, telc, and DSH. Our Intensive plan includes an exam prep guarantee.

What payment methods do you accept? +

Credit card, bank transfer, and PayPal. All prices are in EUR. You'll receive a secure payment link by email within 24 hours of enrolling.
