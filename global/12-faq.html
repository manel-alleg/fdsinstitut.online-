Help

Frequently asked questions.

What level do I need to start?

We welcome students from complete beginners (A1) to advanced speakers (C1/C2). Our placement test helps you find the right group.

How do live sessions work?

Sessions run 3 times per week via Zoom, 90 minutes each. You can also access recordings anytime on our Moodle platform.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?

Yes — pause for up to one month or cancel anytime with 7 days' notice. No penalties.

Do you offer exam preparation?

Yes. We offer dedicated tracks for TestDaF, Goethe-Zertifikat, telc, and DSH exams.

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit card, bank transfer, and PayPal. All prices are in EUR.
