FAQ

Common questions from doctors.

What German level do I need to join?+

We recommend at least B2 general German before starting the C1 Medical track. If you are at B1 or below, start with our general programme — we will transition you automatically when you are ready.

Does the Hueber textbook cost extra?+

No. The Hueber "Deutsch für Ärzte" textbook (digital edition) is included in all plans. A physical copy can be shipped at cost if requested.

How is the Fachsprachprüfung structured?+

The FSP consists of three parts: an anamnesis role-play with a standardised patient, a case presentation to the examining board, and a written Arztbrief (medical letter). Every FDS Medical session trains these exact components.

Can I take this programme if I'm already in Germany?+

Absolutely. Many of our students are already in Germany or Austria on a recognition visa and need to pass the FSP quickly. We adapt session times to your shift schedule.

What if I don't pass the FSP after the course?+

We offer one additional month of intensive preparation at no extra cost for students who do not pass on their first attempt. Our 91% first-attempt pass rate speaks for itself.
